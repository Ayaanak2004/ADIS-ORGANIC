Our Mission

Frequently Asked Questions

We make it easy to explore our programs with no pressure or commitment. Simply complete a quick registration form, and you'll be ready to join us for a day filled with fun, learning, and new experiences.

What types of support services does ADISSUPPORT offer?

We offer a wide range of sporting and recreational programs tailored to individuals with disabilities, including sports activities, skill development sessions, fitness programs, nature adventures, indoor leisure activities, and community outings.

How do I get started with ADISSUPPORT?

Getting started is easy! Simply fill out our contact form or give us a call. We'll schedule an initial consultation to understand your goals and recommend the best programs for you.

Can you help me understand my NDIS funding?

Absolutely! Our team is experienced in working with NDIS participants and can help you understand how to utilize your funding for our programs. We can guide you through the process and answer any questions you may have.

Can you help me with a Support Coordinator?

While we don't provide support coordination services directly, we work closely with many support coordinators and can provide recommendations if needed.

How can I use my NDIS funding for ADISSUPPORT programs?

Our programs are NDIS-approved and can be funded through your Core Supports budget under "Assistance with Social and Community Participation". We can help you with the paperwork and ensure everything is set up correctly.

Do I need to commit all my funding to ADISSUPPORT?

Not at all! We offer flexible program options that allow you to use only the portion of funding you're comfortable with. You can start with one program and expand as you see fit.
